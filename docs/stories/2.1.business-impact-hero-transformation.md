# Story 2.1: Business-Impact Hero Transformation

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As a** business decision-maker visiting Xala's website,
**I want** the hero section to immediately communicate business value and transformation capabilities,
**so that** I can quickly understand how Xala can solve my business challenges.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Hero section prominently displays business transformation messaging instead of technical focus
2. Norwegian content follows Proventus-inspired "Hva kan vi løse for din bedrift?" approach
3. Visual hierarchy emphasizes business outcomes over technical capabilities
4. Call-to-action buttons focus on business consultation rather than generic contact
5. Hero maintains existing visual design excellence and animation quality
6. Business value propositions are clearly communicated within 5 seconds of page load
7. Hero section integrates seamlessly with existing design system and components

# Story 2.2: Customer-Centric Navigation

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As a** business decision-maker,
**I want** navigation that focuses on business challenges and solutions rather than technical categories,
**so that** I can quickly find relevant services that address my specific business needs.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Navigation structure follows customer-centric problem-solution framework
2. Main navigation emphasizes "What can we solve?" approach inspired by Proventus
3. Service categories are presented as business outcomes rather than technical capabilities
4. Navigation includes prominent business consultation pathway
5. Existing navigation functionality and mobile responsiveness is preserved
6. Norwegian navigation follows "Hva kan vi løse for din bedrift?" messaging pattern
7. Navigation integrates seamlessly with existing design system

### Business Content Translation Requirements
**Source: [docs/PRD-Xala-Website-Enhancement.md#functional-requirements]**

**FR7 Implementation**: Norwegian language content will emphasize local market leadership positioning while English content maintains international business appeal.

**Proventus-Inspired Messaging Patterns**:

- **Customer-Centric Approach**: "Hva kan vi løse for din bedrift?"

- **Local Market Leadership**: Emphasis on Norwegian excellence with international standards

### Translation Key Structure Design
**Source: [docs/Technical-Architecture-Xala-Enhancement.md#compatibility-requirements]**

**New Key Categories**:
1. **Business Hero**: `business.hero.*` - Business transformation messaging
2. **Customer Navigation**: `business.navigation.*` - Problem-solution framework
3. **Service Outcomes**: `business.services.*` - Business-focused service descriptions
4. **Success Stories**: `business.stories.*` - Client success and testimonials
5. **Partnership Messaging**: `business.partnership.*` - Collaboration approach content

**Integration Pattern**:
- Extend existing `src/i18n/config.ts` without modifying current structure
- Follow existing nested key patterns for organization
- Maintain backward compatibility with all existing translation keys
